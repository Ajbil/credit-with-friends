# Client sign-off on the CreditWithFriends MVP scope

## Context
Discovery, specs and the roadmap are complete: `docs/product/BRIEF.md`,
accepted decisions 0001 to 0009, five confirmed capability specs (Accounts,
Cards, Circles, Find and contact, Usage metrics) and the derived six-story
roadmap in `plans/roadmap.json` (CWF-1 to CWF-6). Each spec passed its own
cold grill, the roadmap passed the epics grill, and the whole handover passed
the sign-off grill with every finding disposed.

## Decision
Arihant, the client and sole approver, signs off on this MVP scope: a
mobile-first web app where trusted circles list which bank cards they hold, so
a shopper can find a card holder and message them on WhatsApp in one tap, as
specified by the brief, decisions 0001 to 0009, the five confirmed specs and
the six-story roadmap.

## Consequences
- Planning and building may start; architecture docs are written before the
  first story (CWF-1) is planned.
- The scope is the contract: changes need a new or superseding decision, a
  spec change with a fresh grill, or a roadmap change by PR.
- Real members are invited only after all six stories ship (decision 0009).
